# Company Research — Deep Pipeline

Deep company research pipeline adapted from EFRA-AI (Replicant-Partners). A request naming a company starts a sourced background, overview and outlook report; the distinct analytical perspectives supply the substance. An investment-grade judgment is a separate, stricter outcome. MCP tool calls (company_transcript, dcf_valuation, comparable_analysis, web_search, scenario_build) are called directly where applicable; templates synthesize over actual outputs.

## Reference models

The pipeline is converted from EFRA-AI (Replicant-Partners), a project source. The frameworks it applies carry their own authors: Moore, Johnson & Kippola, *The Gorilla Game* (1998) for GORILLA; Wardley, *Wardley Maps* (2016) for the value-chain map (`wardley-mapper`); Simon, *Hidden Champions of the 21st Century* (2009) for SIMON; Rappaport & Mauboussin, *Expectations Investing* (2001) for the expectations gap (`onto_anchor` → derived `expectations_gap`). Derived anchors (operator ruling 2026-09-25): `gorilla_game`, `wardley_map`, `hidden_champion`.

**D/P.** MCP valuation tools and fixed-weight `lisp_eval` GORILLA scoring are D. Board rotation, Wardley evolution, capability assessments, Simon inquiry, IMAGINE scenarios and thesis pillars are judgments grounded in cited evidence and open to contrary cases. An independently verified investment-grade verdict is not inferred from this report.

## When to Use

- When the user asks for a deep background, overview and outlook report on a company, using its name or ticker. No separately supplied research question, ticker, source list, acceptance task or thesis is required; derive the internal work from this request and the actual evidence. For a company without the inputs needed for an investment thesis, produce a sourced qualitative report with explicit gaps rather than an investment_grade verdict.
- When you want the full EFRA-AI research pipeline (COMPANY → GORILLA → IMAGINE → THESIS) as a single governed process.
- When you want the 8-part company analysis (Self-View, Business Franchise, Management Skill, Financial Profile, Invisible Layer, Falstaffian Inversion, Value Gorilla Elevator Pitch, Investment Thesis Statement).
- When you want the GORILLA 4-dimension framework (Obvious Problem / Invisible Gorilla / Combinatorial Solution / Choke Point) with fixed methodology weights.
- When you want IMAGINE's 5/10Y scenarios with digital transformation stage classification and falsifiable predictions persisted to the forecast-ledger.
- When you want the THESIS three-pillar synthesis (Business Franchise, Management Quality, Valuation) with a thesis-judge quality gate.

## When NOT to Use

- A quick take or initiation note — use `company-research-flash` (the flash pipeline; 23 steps vs this 16-step deep pipeline).

- Live trading signals — the deliverable is an investment thesis with a falsifiable prediction set, not an execution signal.

## Instructions

Execution order: collect-evidence → LISTEN → COMPANY → INDUSTRY OUTSIDE VIEW → FALSTAFFIAN → WARDLEY → ECONOMIC TRAJECTORY → GORILLA and capability checks → SIMON → IMAGINE → THESIS where supported → PREPARE full report and summary → report-evidence-review → PERSIST both. Render each named analytical template with actual preceding evidence; a stage result is an interpretation, not an original source.

The analytical perspectives are the point of this deep skill. Retain the distinct company self-view, outside industry economics, competitive rotation, value-chain evolution, falling-cost trajectory, capability-scored opportunity, niche inquiry and 5/10-year alternatives in the final report. Synthesize tensions and contrary cases rather than pasting stage outputs or listing generic risks. Collect evidence once and reuse it across stages. A missing input becomes an explicit limitation of that perspective, not a reason to skip the remaining perspectives or invent a score. Do not spawn a separate agent or run a verification handoff for each stage. No candidate gate or independent verification workflow blocks the requested company overview.

**Extract once.** Collect original documents and keyed verbatim passages once, then reuse a compact evidence digest across the analytical perspectives. A missing fact requires a targeted return to the original, not a new broad search. A company name is the full intake: derive background, business/management/financial overview and conditional outlook without demanding a ticker, extra question or test case. Missing audited inputs limit valuation, not the other perspectives; mark unknowns rather than invent numbers. The report must convey the distinctive conclusions and contradictions each perspective exposes, not paste scores or boilerplate.

### collect-evidence

1. Before COMPANY, identify the company from the user's report request. If a public security is applicable, use `resolve_symbol` with the supplied name or ticker; do not require the user to provide a ticker. If no symbol exists (for example a private company), use its verified official identity for public-source discovery, omit ticker-only tools and mark their unavailable financial/market inputs as gaps. If the name refers to more than one plausible entity and sources cannot disambiguate it, ask only which company was intended; do not ask for an additional research question. Read `forecast_list` and relevant `report_load` only for an applicable resolved symbol, as prior analysis (not primary evidence), and call `begin_research_run`. Pass its `run_id` to each `web_search` / `web_extract` / `web_find_similar` call. Surface ledger failures in `data_gaps`; never claim an unrecorded source was server-observed.
2. Start with how the company sees itself: its own filings, reports and presentations, not sell-side or media reports (MAIA Guidebook, Company Analysis: Where to Start). Retrieve the company description/IR page, the relevant current and prior earnings transcripts, and the historical financial inputs needed by the analysis. Use `company_profile`, `company_transcript`, `income_statement`, `balance_sheet`, `cash_flow_statement`, and `stock_quote` as applicable; fetch the cited filing or original page with `web_extract` before treating a search snippet as support for a load-bearing claim. Keep provider warnings, financial periods, units, and audit status. A search that returns no result is not evidence of market absence.
3. Collect evidence for meaning, not form. Keep original filings, a current and prior management account where available, and independent industry or competitor sources only where they illuminate the business, economics or contrary case. Read audited financial statements for gross-margin durability, working-capital power, invested-capital returns and customer revenue; an unaudited call or partner count is not audited paying-customer evidence. Discard boilerplate unrelated to the economics. For each used original retain its URL, complete file where applicable, deterministic text extraction and a stable `output_key`; keep `source_outputs` separate from synthesis, with period, units and audit status. Failed retrieval stays a named gap. Retain the actual response of a valuation or signpost tool when its calculation reaches the report, distinguish it from audited input and sanity-check the assumptions. Neither a model summary nor a citation alone turns a claim into an original fact.
4. Write the evidence digest (see Extract once) from the retained originals, and pass its records to `company-research/company-8part` for synthesis alongside its existing inputs; retain full originals separately from the digest. Maintain it throughout all later research, including Wardley and the literature probe. Every factual claim's source reference must resolve to a retained record. Missing evidence remains a named gap, not an LLM estimate.
5. Retain only the originals and stage outputs actually used by the report in the run folder, keyed by source and period. Do not create a candidate note, `key-claims.json`, a verification packet, a digest handoff or a second source collection as a condition for analysis. Read `get_research_run` before closeout and disclose ledger failures; the ledger is an audit trail, not a substitute for the original source.

### listen

Read the company the way a long-term owner listens: once, through the `listening` skill's horizon model, so every later stage works from the same cited strategic evidence instead of re-reading raw documents and chasing hype or short-term noise.

1. After collect-evidence, split the company's own narrative originals into verbatim passages of at most ~800 words at paragraph or speaker-turn boundaries: the current and prior earnings-call transcripts, the annual report's business and MD&A sections, and any investor-day or shareholder-letter material retained. Each passage is `{source, entity_ref, text}` with `source` = its `source_outputs.output_key`. Do not pass financial statement tables, notes or boilerplate. A missing prior call is a `data_gaps` entry (management_consistency then rests on the current documents only).
2. Follow the `listening` skill process with `listening/apply-template-rag` (`corpus_passages`, `kg_triples: []` unless a company graph exists, `company_symbol`, `focus_query` = the report's background/overview/outlook scope). Verify every cited quote mechanically against its own passage with `lisp_eval` `string-contains`; drop a quote that fails twice. Record `(list verified total dropped)` in `listening_view`.
3. **Materiality exemption.** LISTEN filters strategic narrative; it is not the materiality check. Audited statements, risk factors, contingencies, commitments and related-party disclosures bypass it and go to `source_outputs` and `disclosure_inventory` unchanged. A disclosure that LISTEN would class `short_term_only` or never retrieved is still material if it bears on the company's economics.
4. Emit `listening_view` (per-section verdicts against the company's own stated strategy, `checkpoint_map`, `strategic_goals`, `ignored_short_term`, counts, `data_gaps`). COMPANY, GORILLA capability checks, IMAGINE, THESIS and prepare-summary consume it instead of the raw narrative text: Self-View and Management Skill quote its verified evidence; the execution-evidence and multi-quarter customer checks use `management_consistency` and the checkpoint map; IMAGINE's predictions and the summary's Future section start from the checkpoint map. Consult an original passage directly only to check a specific quote or fill a named gap. No verdict, score or forecast input may come from `ignored_short_term`. Hype with no dated checkpoint, delivered result or audited support stays context, never corroboration.
5. Keep verbatim passages and the listening view with the run; before quoting a passage in either report, check that the exact wording appears in its keyed original.

### Data Provenance Hierarchy (applies to all steps)

All financial data used in the pipeline must be classified by provenance strength. Stronger provenance overrides weaker provenance when they conflict.

| Level | Source | Legal standing | Use as DCF/valuation input? |
|-------|--------|---------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Audited annual financial statements (in a 10-K or equivalent filing) | Auditor's opinion applies to the financial statements, not every statement in the filing | **Yes — these are the historical anchor** |
| Interim | Financial statements in a 10-Q | Filed, but unaudited interim statements | Cross-check/context; do not label as audited historical inputs |
| 2 | Earnings call transcripts | Regulation FD applies, but forward-looking statements have safe harbor | No — use for qualitative analysis, management assessment, milestone tracking |
| 3 | Press releases / investor presentations | Selective disclosure, not audited | No — use for context, not for financial inputs |
| 4 | Media coverage / analyst reports | Third-party interpretation, may repeat company claims | No — use for incumbent research, market context |
| 5 | Web research / news | Variable quality | No — use for market-reality gate, incumbent verification |

**Historical DCF inputs must come from Level 1 (audited financial statements) only.** Forecast growth, margins and discount rates are explicit assumptions, not audited facts; preserve their rationale and distinguish tool calculations from evidence validating those assumptions. If audited data is insufficient for a meaningful DCF — e.g., pre-revenue company with no historical revenue, margins, or working capital data — the correct output is "insufficient audited data for DCF," not substituting analyst estimates, round-number guesses, or transcript claims as inputs. Swapping in non-audited data as DCF inputs pollutes the calculation and produces a number that has the appearance of rigor without the substance.

**Customer base evidence** must distinguish between:
- Revenue from paying customers (Level 1 — from audited financials)
- Customer/partner counts cited in transcripts (Level 2 — management claim, not audited)
- Partnership announcements in press releases (Level 3 — selective disclosure)

A company citing "60+ MNO partners covering 3B+ subscribers" in a transcript (Level 2) does not constitute evidence of paying customers. Recognized revenue in audited financials supports that sales occurred; it does not verify that every named partner pays, a claimed customer count, retention, or product-level unit economics. Those claims each require their own supporting disclosure.

### company-8part

1. Self-View — how the company describes itself (verbatim, from `listening_view`'s verified evidence and the fetched IR page).
2. Business Franchise — identity, geography, moat, competitive position.
3. Management Skill — CEO scorecard (long-term) + CFO scorecard (working capital) with verbatim transcript quotes.
4. Financial Profile — signposts + 3-stage valuation (consensus → normalization → terminal).
5. Invisible Layer — what's not on the page, what's not in the price.
6. Falstaffian Inversion — pierce market ceremony and social illusion (honour is "air"); find economic life where the consensus disdains to look (the "spare men"); early shoots of improvement the consensus misses.
7. Value Gorilla Elevator Pitch — the economic opportunity, what the company is doing to exploit it, and why it is likely to succeed while the market prices it as if it will not (Guidebook, Value Gorillas), stated only as far as verified evidence supports.
8. Investment Thesis Statement — state whether evidence supports a thesis. If insufficient, say so.
9. Consume company_transcript, dcf_valuation, comparable_analysis, web_search, fetch MCP tool outputs.
10. Emit data_gaps for any failed MCP tool — never collapse to None.
11. **Intent-routed search:** Call `web_search` with the `intent` hint (news, academic, semantic, freshness, general, transcript) and no explicit `provider` — the tool scores the configured providers against (query, intent) and queries the top recommendation as a single-provider call, surfacing the ranking in `provider_recommendations` and the choice in `selected_provider`. This prevents the arxiv-only fallback that occurs when no provider is explicitly selected and the credential map is stale.
12. **Earnings call transcripts:** If `company_transcript` is unavailable, search for `"{company} earnings call transcript Q{N} {year}"` via `web_search(provider="serpapi")` — SerpAPI supports YouTube transcript extraction. Fall back to `web_search(provider="tavily")` for written transcript sources. Never substitute Wikipedia-sourced paraphrases for verbatim management quotes without flagging a `data_gap`.

### Evidence boundary

Keep the cited source, period, units and exact passage for each material historical claim. Check any quotation verbatim against its own retained original and recalculate any material derived number from its cited inputs. A search snippet, model summary, tool valuation or management forecast is not an audited statement. If support is absent or contradictory, omit the assertion or state the uncertainty in the report. This is author-side source discipline, not an independent verification verdict; do not call it verified or compute a fact_score. No candidate packet, independent handoff, Tier 1/Tier 2 score or early gate is required for the requested overview.

### industry-outside-view

An advisory outside view on the industry's business drivers, built from scholarly and institutional sources rather than web opinion. Its searches and retrieval run in wave 1 alongside collect-evidence; its synthesis renders after COMPANY (which anchors on how the company sees itself) and before FALSTAFFIAN. It never blocks and never scores.

1. Name the industry from the company's own self-description (profile and IR page) for the searches; the render step checks it against the COMPANY board's value chain. Query the industry's economics, never the company's name: e.g. `"{industry} cost structure capital intensity pricing power"`, `"{industry} cyclicality entry barriers competition"`, `"{industry} industrial organization market structure"`.
2. Search these tiers, each with explicit `web_search(provider=...)` calls and `run_id`, and log every call's query, provider, `count` and `providers_failed`:
   - Scholarly: `openalex`, `arxiv`, `semantic_scholar` (free) and `google_scholar` (SerpAPI key). Resolve each candidate with `resolve_paper`; an unresolvable identity is excluded. Prefer systematic reviews, meta-analyses and industrial-organization or strategy journals; Google Scholar's `[cited by N]` indicates uptake, not truth.
   - Business-school cases and faculty research: `web_search(provider="serpapi", include_domains=["hbsp.harvard.edu", "thecasecentre.org", "iveypublishing.ca", "gsb.stanford.edu"])` for case catalogues, and `["hbs.edu", "knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu", "insead.edu", "sloanreview.mit.edu"]` for faculty research. A catalogue or author listing is a lead; a case counts only when its own abstract page is retrieved.
   - Books: `google_books` (SerpAPI key). Keep academic or established trade publishers; cite without quoting unless the text itself is retrieved.
   - Industry self-regulatory organizations and standard-setting bodies: search for the industry's SROs, standards bodies and statistical associations (e.g. `"{industry} self-regulatory organization"`, `"{industry} industry standards body"`), then `web_extract` their own published standards, rules or statistics.
   - Substack: admissible only when the author is identified and the retrieved publication page shows more than 1,000 subscribers.
3. Never search Reddit, forums or general web results for this step, and never use answer boxes. Exclude consultant and industry-association reports unless they are in an admissible tier or cited by a scholarly, case or book source. Apply the boilerplate rule: a source with no bearing on this industry's economics is dropped without being listed or counted.
4. Retain each used source as a `source_outputs` record (`source_kind: original` only for retrieved text) and pass short keyed extracts as `source_evidence` plus the call log as `search_log` to `company-research/industry-outside-view`, with `ticker`, `industry` and `company_board`. A failed or empty tier is a `data_gaps` entry, not a block.
5. Before passing the output on, check its field names with `lisp_eval`, binding `view` to the returned JSON object:

   ```lisp
   (let ((has (lambda (k entries) (if (is_null entries) false (if (string= k (car (car entries))) true (has k (cdr entries)))))) (missing (lambda (keys) (if (is_null keys) (list) (if (has (car keys) view) (missing (cdr keys)) (cons (car keys) (missing (cdr keys)))))))) (missing (list "industry_drivers" "self_regulatory_bodies" "company_fit" "frame_conflicts" "implication_for_falstaffian" "implication_for_gorilla" "implication_for_thesis" "claim_sources" "excluded_sources" "data_gaps")))
   ```

   A non-empty result lists missing fields: re-render once with that list; if fields are still missing, pass the view with the gap recorded in `data_gaps`. It never blocks the pipeline.
6. Emit `industry_outside_view`. Pass it to FALSTAFFIAN (frame conflicts), GORILLA (Obvious Problem and Choke Point), THESIS (Business Franchise and Risks).

### falstaffian-competitive-rotation

1. Rotate the competitive framing of the Company Board before GORILLA scores it — structural defense against frame capture by analyst narratives. Render `company-research/falstaffian-competitive-rotation` with `industry_outside_view` (or null) so outside-source frame conflicts are candidate framing errors.
2. Apply Falstaffian semantic rotation shapes (predicate hollow, subject expansion, object inversion, direction reversal) to expose framing errors.
3. Emit rotated_board with competitor-complement analysis, market creator vs participant classification, framing errors detected, and rotated competitive position.
4. GORILLA consumes the rotated board, not the raw Company Board.

### wardley-anchor

1. Compressed value-chain map of the rotated Company Board — compresses wardley-mapper's 6-step process into a single LLM call.
2. **Inventory two classes of components:**
   - **Internal value-chain components** — what the company builds. Classify each on the Wardley evolution axis (Genesis → Custom → Product → Commodity).
   - **Claimed market categories** — every market or capability the company claims to own, create, or be unique in. For each, search via `web_search` for incumbents who already sell it as a product line. Classify based on **what incumbents do**, not what the company claims. If incumbents sell it as a product line, it is at Product/Commodity stage — flag it as a commoditization red flag.
3. Surface commoditization candidates (including any claimed market found to be at Product/Commodity stage), choke points, and invisible gorillas.
4. GORILLA consumes choke_points, invisible_gorillas, AND commoditization_candidates — a claimed market at Product/Commodity stage caps the Obvious Problem score (the company is entering an existing market, not solving a new problem). ECONOMIC TRAJECTORY consumes commoditization_candidates as the falling-cost anchor.
5. **Conditional full Wardley:** If the compressed choke_point score (from GORILLA, evaluated after this step) is < 60, re-run with the full `wardley-mapper` skill (6-step process: inventory → classify → map → movement → recommendations → present). The full map adds movement analysis (component velocity on the evolution axis) and visual positioning that the compressed adapter cannot produce. This conditional trigger avoids convergence fatigue on clear-cut cases while preserving depth when the strategic position is uncertain.

### economic-trajectory

1. Identify the falling-cost trajectory in the subject's industry, the design constraint being removed, the Coasean firm-boundary shifts, and the Kauffman adjacent possible nodes.
2. Consume the Wardley map's commoditization_candidates as the falling-cost anchor — what's becoming commodity IS the falling-cost trajectory.
3. **Strategy-literature-probe:** Before asserting Coasean shifts or adjacent-possible nodes, search for grounding literature via `web_search(provider="exa")` with semantically-framed queries: `"industrial organization market structure entry barriers {industry}"`, `"case study vertical integration {industry} {strategy}"`, `"platform economics two-sided market {business_model}"`. Exa's neural search finds literature by meaning, not keyword. Consume the results as evidence for the trajectory claims — do not assert theoretical grounding without a source. Emit `literature_sources` with each trajectory claim.
4. Emit economic_trajectory with falling_cost, constraint_being_removed, coasean_shifts, adjacent_possible_nodes, convergence_vectors, implications_for_subject, literature_sources.
5. IMAGINE consumes this as the anchor for its 5/10Y scenarios.

### gorilla-4dim

1. **Market-reality gate.** For each market the company claims to own or have created, search via `web_search` for incumbents who sell it as a product line. Output: `claimed market | incumbent | what they sell | URL`. If incumbents exist, cap Obvious Problem at 40. If no incumbents found after searching, state this explicitly and score normally.
2. **Financial red-flag screen.** Answer two questions using Level 1 (audited financial statement) data only:
   - Is capex producing revenue? (Compare capex and revenue trajectories from audited annual statements; use unaudited 10-Q figures only as explicitly labelled interim cross-checks.)
   - Is revenue real? (Check whether reported revenue is from paying customers or from contracts/MoUs. Audited revenue is recognized revenue — not backlog, not contracted, not "committed.")
   If audited data is insufficient to answer either question, state "insufficient audited data" and apply a confidence penalty. If either answer is negative based on audited data, apply a confidence penalty to all GORILLA scores.
3. **Falsification before scoring above 70.** Write one paragraph arguing against any dimension scored above 70. If the counter-argument is more convincing than the supporting evidence, reduce the score.
4. Score the 4 dimensions (Obvious Problem, Invisible Gorilla, Combinatorial Solution, Choke Point) against the ROTATED Company Board, the Wardley map, the economic trajectory, the market-reality gate, and the financial red-flag screen.
5. Every score must cite evidence: transcript quote, web source URL, or financial data point. A score without cited evidence is invalid.
6. Call `lisp_eval` to apply fixed weights (25/30/25/20) and compute the verdict (GORILLA ≥75 / SMALL_ANIMAL 50-74 / PEDESTRIAN <50).
   Pinned form: `(let ((score (+ (* 0.25 obvious_problem) (* 0.30 invisible_gorilla) (* 0.25 combinatorial_solution) (* 0.20 choke_point)))) (cond ((>= score 75) 'GORILLA) ((>= score 50) 'SMALL_ANIMAL) (t 'PEDESTRIAN)))` — env binds each dimension's 0–100 score; maturity-blocked dimensions (from gorilla-capability-reason) bind to 0, not their elicited value.
7. Do NOT propose alternative weightings.

### gorilla-capability-reason

1. Type each GORILLA dimension against a capability registry with floor, ceiling, and maturity-gate limits.
2. **Execution evidence requirement.** For each dimension scored above 50, cite one prior-quarter guidance item or stated milestone from the Company Board and whether it was delivered. The comparison is: `prior guidance | delivered? | evidence`. If delivery is absent across 2+ dimensions, apply a maturity block — the dimension contributes 0 to the fixed-weight score, not its elicited value; retain the explicit maturity block and evidence gap separately. This distinguishes companies that see an opportunity and are executing from companies that see an opportunity and are not.
3. **Customer base evidence requirement.** The most valuable asset any business has is its paying customer base (Reichheld: loyal, low-turnover customers drive ROIC by reducing acquisition costs and enabling referral-driven growth). For each dimension scored above 50, cite evidence of paying customers using the Data Provenance Hierarchy:
   - **Paying customers (Level 1 — audited)**: recognized revenue from audited annual statements. Customer counts need their own disclosure and audit-status check; location in a filing alone does not make a count audited. Calculate ARPU only from definition- and period-aligned sourced revenue and counts. Unaudited 10-Q figures remain labelled interim context.
   - **Non-paying relationships (Level 2-3 — unaudited)**: partnerships, MoUs, letters of intent, "agreements" cited in transcripts or press releases that have not converted to recognized revenue
   If the company has no Level 1 evidence of paying customers but claims a large partner ecosystem (Level 2-3), apply a maturity block on Obvious Problem and Combinatorial Solution. A company that cannot get people to pay for its product has not demonstrated the capability to monetize the opportunity, regardless of opportunity size.
   Cross-reference `listening_view` (from LISTEN: `management_consistency` and the checkpoint map) for multi-quarter trajectory: are customer counts growing? Are partners converting to paying customers? Is revenue per customer growing? Persistent partner counts without revenue conversion is a signal that the company sees the opportunity but cannot execute on the most basic business function.
4. Evaluate whether the GORILLA scores are defensible given the company's capability maturity, execution track record, and customer base evidence.
5. Emit floor_violations, ceiling_violations, maturity_blocks. Finalize the fixed-weight GORILLA score only after these checks; earlier scores are provisional. A maturity block contributes 0 for that dimension in the `lisp_eval` scoring call; it remains an explicit block in the assessment, not a zero-valued measurement.

### hidden-champions-inquiry

1. After GORILLA's capability check, render `company-research/hidden-champions-inquiry` with `ticker`, `rotated_board`, `wardley_map`, `source_evidence` and `demand_context` (null unless a dated, category-matched demand source is retained). Construct `source_evidence` as short extracts keyed by the original `source_outputs.output_key` with URL, period and unit; keep full originals in `source_outputs` for report citations. Search for independent niche-market and competitor evidence if a leadership claim matters to the thesis; record all retrieved originals and failures with the run.
2. Use Hermann Simon's *Hidden Champions of the 21st Century* as a flexible inquiry into the customer's need, narrow market boundary, focus, deep solution, customer proximity, innovation, globalization and the tradeoff between niche leadership and market saturation. These are observed patterns, **not** a required eight-item checklist, revenue limit, score or investment gate. The existing fixed-weight GORILLA verdict remains untouched.
3. Emit `simon_inquiry`: a source-keyed niche definition, `supported | contested | unknown` **leadership claim**, mechanism and contrary evidence, concentration and growth options, exceptions, implications for scenarios/thesis, and data gaps. A share requires compatible numerator, denominator, geography and period; management's “number one” alone is not verification. Distinguish company integrity and price expectations from this strategic lens. Missing source evidence yields `unknown`, never a favorable classification or automatic exclusion.
4. Pass `simon_inquiry` (or explicit null with the named failure in `data_gaps`) to `company-research/imagine-longrange` and `company-research/thesis-three-pillars`. Pass `industry_outside_view` (or null) to `company-research/gorilla-4dim` and `company-research/thesis-three-pillars` the same way. Reconcile it with Wardley and Falstaffian findings; contradictions remain visible in the report. A favorable niche inquiry does not make an investment-grade verdict.

### imagine-longrange

1. Classify the digital transformation stage (MODEL / SHADOW / TWIN / SOURCE).
2. Classify the growth driver (innovation / demographic / both / neither).
3. Construct 5/10Y scenarios using the scenario_build MCP tool output as scaffold, ANCHORED on the economic trajectory and CHALLENGED by the Falstaffian rotations. When `simon_inquiry` is present, test whether niche saturation, adjacent demand or disputed leadership changes a scenario; do not assume a company fits every Simon trait.
4. Emit 3–5 falsifiable predictions tagged by horizon (5Y/10Y) for the forecast-ledger — the IMAGINE kata loop. Each prediction includes its own rationale and retained `source_outputs.output_key` references, separate from the board-level rationale. An unsupported rationale records a `data_gaps` entry rather than an invented base rate.
5. Emit what's not on the page (anchored on the adjacent possible) and what's not in the price (anchored on the trajectory's implications).

### eqm-forecast-rationale-improvement (optional)

1. After IMAGINE, choose at most **one** load-bearing dated yes/no prediction whose `statement`, `probability`, `resolution_criteria`, `expiration_date`, `rationale` and `rationale_sources` are present. Preserve the original IMAGINE board and any persisted forecast snapshot. A thesis paragraph without a resolvable probability is **not** an EQM forecast; skip the pass with a named `eqm_review.data_gaps` entry if no eligible prediction or no accessible EQM scoring tool.
2. Follow the `eqm` skill's Improve process for **one** in-session experiment: score the original rationale with `market_score_rationale(question, forecast_probability, rationale)`; if scoring fails, `missing_eqms` is non-empty, or the returned marker IDs do not cover the selected predictive-12 catalog when `missing_eqms` is absent, retain the original and surface the reason. Render the EQM Improve templates in order with their declared inputs: `eqm/eqm-imp-direction` gets original `rationale`, `forecast_probability`, `question` and null optional targets; `eqm/eqm-imp-current` gets those plus `direction` and uses the actual tool scores; `eqm/eqm-imp-target` gets `current_composite`, `failing_markers`, `red_flag_screen` and null optional targets; `eqm/eqm-imp-predict` gets its `target_condition`, `prioritized_markers`, `current_composite`, `target_composite_score`; `eqm/eqm-imp-experiment` gets original `rationale`, `forecast_probability`, `question`, `prediction` as an object containing the original forecast snapshot, `eqm-imp-predict`'s intervention and short verified evidence extracts keyed to retained `source_outputs`, plus `target_condition` and marker descriptions. Never supply invented score fields just to satisfy a template. Record the predicted marker intervention with `kanban_goal_create` as directed by EQM Improve, then judge the measured marker change after re-scoring; only the operator can confirm the goal outcome. This marker prediction is process feedback, not a claim that forecast accuracy improves.
3. Ground any added evidence in an original retrieved source and append it to `source_outputs`; obtain a real opposing case rather than inserting marker phrases. Keep the `statement`, `horizon`, `probability`, `resolution_criteria`, `expiration_date`, `trajectory_basis` and `rationale_sources` fixed. The only allowed working-copy edit is the selected prediction's `rationale`. New source references belong in `eqm_review.source_refs`, not a silent modification of the prediction. If new evidence contradicts COMPANY or IMAGINE, re-enter the earliest affected stage within the existing three-iteration bound, not just polish the wording.
4. Re-score the proposed rationale once; render `eqm/eqm-validate` only when a sufficiently sized, matched resolved-forecast cohort supplies its required `forecaster_level_composite`, `realized_outcomes` and `per_rationale_scores` inputs. A single outcome cannot establish a correlation; otherwise record `Undetermined`. With no realized outcome, record `Undetermined` and do **not** present a score gain as improved forecast accuracy. Reject score-gaming, changed load-bearing claims, unsupported additions or probability misalignment; otherwise construct the revised IMAGINE working view by copying the original prediction and replacing only `rationale`. Never overwrite a persisted forecast. Record before/after text, source keys, scoring caveats and `status: proposed | rejected | skipped` with `data_gaps` in `eqm_review`; retain the original if skipped or rejected. A proposed rationale is a model inference, not a historical fact. This pass is advisory, not a thesis publication gate or an EQM minimum-score threshold.
5. Keep the revised IMAGINE working view and `eqm_review` (including skipped/failed status and gaps) with the scenario record and the final report evidence. Do not pass it into `company-research/thesis-three-pillars`: expected events are part of the scenario process, not the investment thesis (Guidebook, Investment Thesis). Check new factual wording against its cited original; thesis-judge remains a separate investment-grade decision only when requested.

### thesis-three-pillars

1. **Before rendering THESIS**, explicitly answer: is this a value gorilla at the current, as-of-dated price, and why or why not? Record `value_gorilla_judgment` as a source-keyed answer (`supported | contested | undetermined`, reasons and contrary evidence), using the final GORILLA capability result, paying-customer evidence, market price and IMAGINE 10Y scenario. If a price or necessary evidence is missing, say `undetermined`; never invent it. This is a pre-draft investment question, not a self-assigned `investment_grade` / `needs_work` / `incomplete` verdict or a substitute for thesis-judge. Pass the answer and the run's `as_of_date` to `company-research/thesis-three-pillars`.
2. Synthesize all prior research into a formal investment thesis covering the three pillars: Business Franchise (moat strength, value creation, durability), Management Quality (capital allocation, leadership), Valuation (3-stage: consensus → normalization → terminal). Consume `simon_inquiry` as advisory evidence about a customer-defined niche and its exceptions, not a hidden-champion verdict; unsupported share claims stay unknown. State an explicit price conclusion at the as-of price (attractive, unattractive or indeterminate and why), reconciled with IMAGINE 10Y and the terminal stage; a numeric price target is not required. Do not silently resolve an inconsistency between price and the 10Y scenario.
3. Keep the thesis (all three pillars, statement and rationale) in the time-relative present: qualify current claims by the as-of date; keep dated forecasts, management guidance and add/trim price levels in the report's Outlook section only, not in THESIS (IMAGINE is an internal input to Outlook). Do not copy future events into the ownership thesis.
4. **Before accepting THESIS**, run a mechanical precheck over every thesis field: require the pre-draft answer and nonempty explicit price conclusion and IMAGINE 10Y reconciliation; use `lisp_eval` for non-null/nonempty `value_gorilla_judgment`, `valuation.price_conclusion`, `valuation.narrative` and `thesis_statement`, plus `string-contains` for literal `guidance`/`forecast`; use a case-insensitive regex scan over the joined thesis fields with `(?i)(20[0-9]{2}.{0,80}(forecast|project|expect)|(forecast|project|expect).{0,80}20[0-9]{2}|guidance|(add|trim|buy below|sell above).{0,40}[$€£][0-9])` to flag dated projections, guidance and add/trim price levels. Qualify historical dates and flag false positives for review rather than deleting sourced facts blindly. Flag all hits for review; regex alone cannot establish meaning or detect every paraphrase. Inspect and remove any actual forbidden content, check as-of qualification and the three-pillar statement by reading the text, then rerun the precheck until clean. An unresolved hit or missing 10Y evidence is a named gap, not a mechanical pass or a fabricated thesis. Repeat this check after essentialist edits and before prepare-summary.
5. Do NOT self-evaluate — the investment_grade / needs_work / incomplete verdict comes from the cross-skill company-research/thesis-judge step in the process.

### thesis-essentialist

1. Run a single pass of the 3-gate eliminative interrogation (Exist, Surface, Contract) on the thesis.
2. Gate 1 (Exist): delete each pillar — does the thesis collapse? If not, the pillar is decorative.
3. Gate 2 (Surface): count load-bearing claims — ≤ 7 passes, > 7 requires justification.
4. Gate 3 (Contract): trace abstractions — are moat source, durability, and terminal stage genuine content or labels/hedges?
5. The elimination_report feeds the thesis-judge quality gate as additional evidence — it does not block the thesis directly.

### prepare-summary

1. Write the full report from the distinct COMPANY, LISTEN, industry, FALSTAFFIAN, WARDLEY, trajectory, GORILLA, SIMON and IMAGINE outputs. State each perspective as a company-specific conclusion with its mechanism, best opposing case, source and implication for the outlook; reconcile disagreements rather than concatenating template responses. Include financial signposts and valuation only where historical audited inputs and modeled assumptions support them. If THESIS cannot support a price conclusion, state the valuation gap without inventing a target or dropping the other perspectives.
2. Write the MAIA team summary from that report: Name, Website, As of Date, Business, Management, Valuation or its gap, Risks and Future. Keep it under 1,000 words. The summary is a shorter argument, not another pile of disclaimers or an independent conclusion. Place forecast checkpoints and management guidance in Outlook, not as timeless thesis facts. Every material sourced factual statement in both files has an original URL or output key; a model judgment is labelled as such.

### report-evidence-review

Before publication, read the full report and summary against the actual cited originals: quote wording, financial period and units, disclosed material risks and contrary facts. Correct unsupported wording once; if evidence is unavailable, explicitly qualify the claim and publish the source-qualified report with its gaps. The same check applies to every factual line in both outputs. This is not a remote verifier, a packet digest, a fabricated fact score or permission to label the report independently verified.

### persist-report

1. After the report-evidence-review, publish both rich markdown files, preserving the cited factual text and openly naming material evidence gaps. Label them SOURCE-QUALIFIED, NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED; investment_grade is NOT ASSESSED unless the user separately requested and completed the stricter judgment. Never claim a mechanical quote match independently verified the report.
2. Reports are stored in the user-facing artifacts directory: `~/Documents/zk-data/companies-mcp/reports/`. This is separate from the internal data dir (`~/.local/share/zed-kask/`) — reports are user-facing artifacts that should be visible, not buried in a hidden cache directory.
3. Create the reports directory if it does not exist: `mkdir -p ~/Documents/zk-data/companies-mcp/reports` via `terminal`.
4. Write the markdown file to `~/Documents/zk-data/companies-mcp/reports/{ticker-or-company-slug}-{date}.md` for single-company analyses, or the corresponding two-identifier path for comparisons. Use a resolved ticker when one exists; otherwise derive the path-safe slug from the verified company name, never invent a listing. Use a quoted `terminal` heredoc for this external artifacts path; project file tools cannot write outside the workspace.
5. The markdown file is the deliverable — full rich markdown with all sections, mermaid diagrams, source notes, and citations.
6. Do NOT write reports to the source tree (`zed-kask/reports/` or similar) — that pollutes the user's code repository.
7. Do NOT write reports to the hidden internal data dir (`~/.local/share/zed-kask/mcp/companies/reports/`) — that buries user-facing output where the user will never find it.
8. Clean up (storage Cleanup rule). A revised report replaces the earlier file for the same company and date: write the new version at the same path rather than a `-v2` beside it. Delete working drafts, scratch evidence and a superseded version once the new report is persisted; keep an evidence folder only when the report cites files in it, and name it in the run summary. Never delete another company's or another date's report.

### condense-report

1. The full markdown report and the MAIA team write-up prepared in `prepare-summary` are the two deliverables. This step exports the already drafted summary to `~/Documents/zk-data/companies-mcp/reports/{ticker-or-company-slug}-summary-{date}.md`.
2. Both files live in `~/Documents/zk-data/companies-mcp/reports/` and carry the same provenance label. If the summary introduces a new factual statement, check it against the original before release; otherwise remove it.

## Convergence

The default deep skill is complete only when the two published files answer the company-name request with all applicable analytical perspectives, grounded factual claims, concrete tensions and a conditional outlook. ## Cross-Skill Composition

- LISTEN reuses the `listening` skill (`listening/apply-template-rag`) over the company's own narrative documents once, after collect-evidence; its verified `listening_view` replaces raw-narrative reading downstream, with audited statements and material disclosures exempt from its horizon filter.
- FALSTAFFIAN reuses the falstaffian perspective-engine shapes and decision tree (via `company-research/falstaffian-competitive-rotation`).
- WARDLEY compresses `wardley-mapper`'s 6-step process via the `company-research/wardley-anchor` adapter. Conditionally upgrades to full `wardley-mapper` when choke_point score < 60.
- ECONOMIC TRAJECTORY includes a strategy-literature-probe via Exa semantic search for IO/competition economics grounding.
- GORILLA runs `company-research/gorilla-capability-reason` — types each GORILLA dimension against a capability registry with floor/ceiling/maturity-gate limits. Dimensions with explicit maturity blocks contribute 0 in the fixed-weight `lisp_eval` score; missing source data remains a surfaced gap.
- SIMON renders `company-research/hidden-champions-inquiry` as an advisory Simon lens over the rotated company framing and Wardley map; its source-keyed output reaches IMAGINE and THESIS without modifying GORILLA weights or the independent integrity/valuation gates.
- EQM after IMAGINE optionally composes `eqm` Improve over one IMAGINE dated forecast: `market_score_rationale` before/after, source-grounded single rewrite via `eqm/eqm-imp-experiment`, preserve all forecast fields except rationale, `Undetermined` when no outcomes, and a visible skip/failure record. THESIS consumes `eqm_review`; neither its score nor polish overrides the late factual and semantic gates.
- THESIS essentialist review reuses `essentialist/essentialist-flow` (via `company-research/thesis-essentialist` adapter) — runs a single pass of the 3-gate eliminative interrogation (Exist, Surface, Contract) on the thesis to enforce parsimony. The elimination_report feeds the thesis-judge quality gate as additional evidence.
- The semantic quality gate reuses `company-research/thesis-judge` (semantic evaluation of the thesis against the three-pillar investment_grade criteria). This avoids the LLM-improves-against-LLM-scored-target trap per .rules — the quality gate is grounded in the thesis-judge semantic evaluator, not LLM self-assessment.
- PREPARE-SUMMARY condenses the analytical conclusions without adding uncited facts; PERSIST/CONDENSE export the full report and its aligned summary to `~/Documents/zk-data/companies-mcp/reports/`.

## Constraints

- The full report is the deliverable: company-specific, sourced conclusions from the distinct analytical lenses, not a clipping collection or a compliance checklist.
- Do not claim independent verification, a fact score or investment grade from author-side source checks. A separate investment-grade request needs its own real judgment; failure to perform it is NOT ASSESSED.
- A historical DCF input requires audited annual financial statements. Forecast assumptions, interim figures, management claims and model outputs are labelled at their actual provenance level.
- Failed tools and missing evidence stay visible as data gaps; they do not collapse to invented numbers or suppress the requested overview and outlook.
- Cite material factual assertions and check quoted text against its retained original before publishing both report and summary.
