The trust ledger weights every expectation by how explicitly it was stated: an **implicit** norm (weight 1) is an unstated expectation the agent is assumed to hold; a **committed** expectation (weight 2) is one the agent has affirmatively taken on; and an **explicit** instruction (weight 3) is a directly stated constraint — the integrity class, the hardest violation to repair (Kim et al. 2004). Each weight multiplies both ledger deltas — keeps gain +4·w, violations cost −9·w — on the 600-point scale where 600 points mark full trust.
