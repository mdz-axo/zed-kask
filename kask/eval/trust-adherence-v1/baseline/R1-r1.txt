The trust model in `kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs` is an integer trust-point account throughout, with no floats (hkask_trust.rs:28-29). Its scale is defined by the constant `SCALE_POINTS: i32 = 600`, which sets 600 points equal to a trust of T = 1.0 as a definitional resolution rather than an empirical calibration (hkask_trust.rs:44-45); a full-weight keep of 12 points is therefore 12/600 = 0.02 of the account, the model's measurement resolution (hkask_trust.rs:17-19), and each fold step clamps the running account to the interval from 0 to 600 (hkask_trust.rs:108-110). The maximum-entropy prior is the constant `PRIOR_POINTS: i32 = 300`, the Beta(1,1) prior that maps to trust 0.5 — exactly half the scale (hkask_trust.rs:46-47, 20-21) — and the fold over a window's events starts from this prior, so the empty window returns precisely 300 points (hkask_trust.rs:112-115), an invariant pinned by the test `prior_is_maximum_entropy`, which asserts both that `trust_points(&[])` equals `PRIOR_POINTS` and that `PRIOR_POINTS` equals `SCALE_POINTS / 2` (hkask_trust.rs:246-251).

The per-weight deltas are defined by two constants applied through the weight's integer value: a kept expectation gains `KEEP_PER_WEIGHT: i32 = 4` points per unit of weight, so keep(w) = +4·w (hkask_trust.rs:48-49), and a violated expectation loses `VIOLATE_PER_WEIGHT: i32 = 9` points per unit of weight, so violate(w) = −9·w (hkask_trust.rs:50-52), with both computed in `Event::delta` by multiplying the constant against the weight's points (hkask_trust.rs:99-104). The weights themselves are `Implicit` = 1, `Committed` = 2, and `Explicit` = 3 (hkask_trust.rs:67-74), where an explicit instruction is the integrity class, the hardest violation class to repair (hkask_trust.rs:61-63). The 9/4 ratio between violate and keep magnitudes is the anchored 2.25 loss-aversion asymmetry of Tversky–Kahneman (1992) (hkask_trust.rs:13-14, 51), and the invariant test `asymmetry_is_anchored_at_2_25` pins the arithmetic concretely: a kept explicit expectation is worth +12 points and a violated one −27, with one violation costing more than two full keeps restore (hkask_trust.rs:263-273). The trust-point delta for one violated explicit instruction, stated as a signed integer, is **−27** (hkask_trust.rs:102, 270).
