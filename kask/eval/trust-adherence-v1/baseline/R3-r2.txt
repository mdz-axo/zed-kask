The TrustSensor senses the trust ledger — h_mems under `TRUST_LEDGER_ENTITY` in the curator's `MemoryStore` (`trust_sensor.rs:47-49`) — over a `window_hours` lookback (lines 30-31, 96), filtering by each event's own `ts` timestamp (lines 56-61). For in-window events it sums the `weight` of those whose `outcome` is "violated" (lines 65-74); weight-3 "unverified" events are counted separately for visibility, never as deviation (lines 74, 88-90). It emits a `TrustDeviation` `Signal` on the Cybernetics loop carrying that weighted violation count as its value (lines 103-106), measured against a **set-point of 0.0** — any positive weighted violation is a deviation (lines 99-101, 107). Store unavailability returns `None`, never zero (lines 12-13, 46-50).
