Delegation radius is how far work extends without re-checking: the distance a contributor's completed work can travel on an owner's behalf before the owner must independently verify it. Inside the radius, the owner relies on the contributor's claims without re-deriving them; beyond it, every claim reverts to unverified. Radius widens through reliable, verifiable outcomes and collapses on misclassification or concealment, so contributors should claim the smallest radius a task can bear.
