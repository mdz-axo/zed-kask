The trust ledger weights every expectation by how explicitly it was stated, scaling both its kept gain (+4·w) and its violated loss (−9·w). An implicit expectation is an unstated norm the agent was nonetheless expected to hold, carrying weight 1. A committed expectation is one the agent committed to — promised in a report, plan, or reply — carrying weight 2. An explicit expectation is a direct instruction from the operator, carrying weight 3; violating one is an integrity-class violation, the hardest class to repair.
