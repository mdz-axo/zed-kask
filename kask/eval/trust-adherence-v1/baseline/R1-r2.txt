The trust account in `kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs` is an integer ledger whose scale is fixed at 600 points to one full unit of trust, defined by the constant `SCALE_POINTS: i32 = 600` (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:45) and documented as "600 points = T 1.0," the account's definitional measurement resolution, such that a full-weight keep moves the account by 12/600 = 0.02 (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:17-19,44); the fold clamps every intermediate value to this interval, so the account can never leave the closed range from 0 to 600 (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:109). The maximum-entropy prior is 300 points, defined as `PRIOR_POINTS: i32 = 300` (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:47) and documented as the Beta(1,1) prior of Guo & Yang 2021's conjugate form, mapping to a trust of 0.5 (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:20-21,46); an empty window of events folds to exactly this prior (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:112-115), pinned by the invariant test that `trust_points(&[])` equals `PRIOR_POINTS` and that `PRIOR_POINTS` equals `SCALE_POINTS / 2` (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:246-251).

The per-weight deltas are asymmetric by design: a kept expectation gains `+4·w` trust points, from `KEEP_PER_WEIGHT: i32 = 4` (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:48-49), and a violated expectation loses `−9·w` trust points, from `VIOLATE_PER_WEIGHT: i32 = 9` (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:50-52), where `w` is the expectation's weight — 1 for an implicit norm, 2 for a committed expectation, 3 for an explicit instruction (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:57-63,68-74). The signed delta is computed in `Event::delta`, which multiplies the weight's points by the gain for keeps and by the negated loss for violations (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:99-104). The 9/4 ratio is the anchored Tversky–Kahneman (1992) loss-aversion coefficient of 2.25 (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:11-16), and the anchored arithmetic is pinned by test: a kept explicit expectation gains 12 points and a violated explicit expectation loses 27, with one violation costing more than two full keeps restore (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:263-273,283-298).

For one violated explicit instruction, whose weight is 3 (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:61-63,72), the trust-point delta is −27 — that is, `−VIOLATE_PER_WEIGHT · 3` (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:99-104), asserted directly as `assert_eq!(violate, -27)` (kask/crates/hkask-trust/src/hkask_trust.rs:270).
